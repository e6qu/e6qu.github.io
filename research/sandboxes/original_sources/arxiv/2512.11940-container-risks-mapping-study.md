## Summary
A systematic mapping study of 129 primary studies on container security. It organises risks and vulnerabilities across the container life cycle into a new taxonomy, with causes, implications, mitigations, and an inventory of security practices and tools. Useful as a recent survey entry point; it cites the older, widely used Sultan et al. (2019) survey, which is not on arXiv.

## Key points
- 129 primary studies; taxonomy of risks across the container life cycle.
- Lists mitigation techniques, security practices and tools.
- Calls for more software-engineering research on container security practice.

## Excerpts
> "we conducted a Systematic Mapping Study (SMS) based on 129 selected primary studies to explore and organize existing knowledge on security issues in software container systems." (Abstract)

## Relevance to the talk
Background citation for "containers share a kernel, and the literature has catalogued how that goes wrong". For the classic survey, cite Sultan, Ahmad, Dimitriou, "Container Security: Issues, Challenges, and the Road Ahead", IEEE Access 7 (2019), DOI 10.1109/ACCESS.2019.2911732 (not an arXiv paper).
