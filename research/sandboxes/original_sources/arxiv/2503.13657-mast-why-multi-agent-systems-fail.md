## Summary
MAST, a taxonomy of 14 failure modes in 3 categories, built from annotated traces of multi-agent
LLM systems (human inter-annotator Cohen's kappa 0.88). An o1-based LLM annotator, given the
trace, the definitions and few-shot examples (not the outcome), reached kappa 0.77 with humans.

## Failure modes
- FC1 System Design Issues: FM-1.1 Disobey task specification; FM-1.2 Disobey role
  specification; FM-1.3 Step repetition; FM-1.4 Loss of conversation history; FM-1.5 Unaware of
  termination conditions.
- FC2 Inter-Agent Misalignment: FM-2.1 Conversation reset; FM-2.2 Fail to ask for
  clarification; FM-2.3 Task derailment; FM-2.4 Information withholding; FM-2.5 Ignored other
  agent's input; FM-2.6 Reasoning-action mismatch.
- FC3 Task Verification: FM-3.1 Premature termination; FM-3.2 No or incomplete verification;
  FM-3.3 Incorrect verification.

## Excerpts (Appendix A)
> Step repetition: "Unnecessary reiteration of previously completed steps in a process, potentially causing delays or errors in task completion."

> Unaware of termination conditions: "Lack of recognition or understanding of the criteria that should trigger the termination of the agents' interaction, potentially leading to unnecessary continuation."

> Premature termination: "Ending a dialogue, interaction or task before all necessary information has been exchanged or objectives have been met, potentially resulting in incomplete or incorrect outcomes."

> Fail to ask for clarification: "Inability to request additional information when faced with unclear or incomplete data, potentially resulting in incorrect actions."

> Task derailment: "Deviation from the intended objective or focus of a given task, potentially resulting in irrelevant or unproductive actions."

## Relevance to the talk
The questions an agent judge asks of a trace: where the agent got lost (derailment), did useless
work (step repetition, not knowing when to stop), stopped too early, or failed to ask.
