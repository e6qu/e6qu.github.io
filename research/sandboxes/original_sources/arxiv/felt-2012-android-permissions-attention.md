## Summary
Install-time permission screens on Android were meant to let users cancel over-privileged apps. An Internet survey (308 users) and a lab study (25 users) found that only 17% paid attention to permissions during installation, and only 3% could answer all three comprehension questions. In the lab, 42% did not know permissions existed.

## Key points
- 17% attention rate in both the survey and the lab study.
- 3% of survey respondents answered all three comprehension questions correctly.
- 42% of lab participants were unaware of permissions at all.
- A minority did read and understand them, so the mechanism is not useless, only weak as the main defence.
