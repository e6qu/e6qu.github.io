## Summary
Characterises the full production FaaS workload of Azure Functions. Most functions are invoked rarely, but invocation frequency spans eight orders of magnitude, so keeping every sandbox warm is wasteful while cold-starting is slow. Proposes a practical keep-alive/pre-warm policy that reduces cold starts using fewer resources. Dataset published (AzurePublicDataset).

## Key points
- Most functions invoked very infrequently; 8-order-of-magnitude range in invocation frequency.
- Workload-aware resource management policy reduces cold starts while spending fewer resources.

Economic background for why sandbox startup time and idle cost dominate platform design; agent sandboxes (idle while the model thinks) have a similar warm-vs-cold dilemma.
