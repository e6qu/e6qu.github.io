## Summary
Apple moved the Mac App Store sandboxing deadline from 2012-03-01 to 2012-06-01. The stated reason was
to give developers time to adopt new entitlements in OS X 10.7.3 and new APIs in Xcode 4.3.

## Key points
- The effective date of mandatory sandboxing for new Mac App Store submissions became 2012-06-01.
- New entitlements in 10.7.3 show the first-cut entitlement set was too coarse for real apps.

## Excerpts
> "We have extended the deadline for sandboxing your apps on the Mac App Store from March 1st to June 1st to provide you with enough time to take advantage of new sandboxing entitlements available in OS X 10.7.3 and new APIs in Xcode 4.3."

## Relevance to the talk
A small but telling data point: retrofitting a sandbox onto existing desktop software meant moving the
deadline and adding entitlements. The same tension shows up today when agent sandboxes break developer
tools.
