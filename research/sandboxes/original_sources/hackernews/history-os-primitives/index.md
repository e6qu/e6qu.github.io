# Hacker News index — sandboxing history and OS isolation primitives

Exhaustive raw list of Hacker News stories on the history of sandboxing
and OS isolation primitives, collected 2026-11-22 for the sandboxing talk.
Notes on the most significant threads are listed in [README.md](README.md);
stories with a note are marked **note** below.

**Counts:** 1048 stories (780 with ≥20 points), 22 thread notes.

**Format:** `date · points · comments · [title](HN thread) · [link](story URL)`.
Groups are ordered by topic; stories are newest first within a group. A
story appears once, in the first group that matches. Square brackets in
titles are shown as parentheses.

**Method.** HN Algolia API (`hn.algolia.com/api/v1/search`, `tags=story`,
`typoTolerance=false`), 355 query variants: each run once with
`numericFilters=points>=20` (paginated, up to 400 hits) and most also run
unfiltered (top 100 by relevance) to catch low-scoring primary sources.
Hits were assigned to groups by title regex, then pruned by hand to remove
false matches (jail sentences, time zones, the NaCl crypto library, PHP and
TypeScript namespaces, app launches that merely ship as Docker images, macOS
and agent-sandbox stories covered by other topics). Stories under 20 points
are kept when they are primary sources or on the history topics (≥5
points) or otherwise ≥10 points. About 25 stories found by hand or from
comment searches were added. Points and comment counts are as returned by
Algolia on 2026-11-22.

Known gaps: Algolia matches titles, URLs and story text, so a story whose
title does not name the primitive (for example a blog post titled after a
CVE nickname) can be missed; HN has almost no stories on Janus, systrace,
AIX WPAR or Linux-VServer, and only a handful (≤4 points) on JEP 411.
