## Summary
Sketch leaving the Mac App Store, citing review times and "technical limitations imposed by the Mac App Store
guidelines (sandboxing and so on)". The thread is the high-water mark of the "sandboxing drove developers off
the MAS" narrative. Related: Atlassian's 2012 "our decision to abandon the Mac App Store" (372 points,
https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=3599991).

## Key points
- Sandboxing named alongside review delays and no upgrade pricing as reasons to leave.
- Pre-mandate apps could stay but only receive bug fixes, no new features, unless they adopted the sandbox.
- Some argue games and simple apps sandbox easily; general desktop tools do not.
- Several commenters already compare the sandboxed-app model to containers/unikernels.

## Relevance to the talk
A cautionary data point: an OS sandbox imposed as a distribution requirement, with entitlements that did not
fit real workloads, pushed developers out rather than in. Agent sandboxes face the same "usability or
adoption" trade-off.
